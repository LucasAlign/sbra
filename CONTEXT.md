# Collab network

Collab connects local business communities through shared business identity and relationships. Chambers and other community operators retain their brands and membership relationships.

## Language

**Person**: A human with one Collab identity who may represent several organizations and participate in several communities.
_Avoid_: Member as a synonym for identity.

**Organization**: A canonical business, chamber, association, municipality, or other institution. A business is an organization, and a chamber is both an organization and a potential community operator.

**Organization affiliation**: A person's relationship with an organization, including their title and authority to represent it.

**Community**: A branded gathering of people and organizations around geography, an operator, or a shared interest, and the primary tenant boundary for membership administration, settings, and private community data.
_Avoid_: Organization as a synonym for community.

**Tenant**: An independently administered community that controls its own branding, settings, membership relationships, and private records. Tenants do not own the shared identities of participating people or businesses.

**Community membership**: A person's or organization's relationship with a particular community, with its own status, type, join date, dues, and private administrative information controlled by that community.

**Community invitation**: A time-limited offer of ordinary membership addressed to a specific person, which that person must accept. It confers no authority to represent or administer a business.

**Region**: A geographic area used to organize discovery and community coverage, optionally serving as an administrative parent with explicitly delegated authority and permitted aggregate reporting. Berks County is the first region; geographic inclusion alone conveys no access to community-private data.

**Shared network data**: Canonical person and organization identities and profile information available under their publication and access preferences. Shared identity does not make every identity field public.

**Tenant-owned relationship data**: Community-specific membership information, dues, internal notes, tags, committee participation, and private communications controlled by the community that owns the relationship.

**Network**: The interconnected collection of Collab communities and regions with shared identity and agreed discovery rules.

**Opportunity**: A business need, offer, or collaboration request with a desired outcome and an audience.

**Introduction**: A proposed connection between people that requires recipient acceptance before contact details are shared.

**Connection**: An accepted relationship between people, distinct from the private notes each participant keeps about it.

**Publication**: An explicit choice to make an item discoverable to a particular audience; it does not transfer ownership of the item.
