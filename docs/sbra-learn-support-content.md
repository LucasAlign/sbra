## Scope and source of truth

The canonical public website is [https://www.sbrassociation.com/](https://www.sbrassociation.com/). This is the domain used by the live site, its canonical tags, navigation, contact details, and [XML sitemap](https://www.sbrassociation.com/sitemap.xml). Some legal copy and the old podcast feed still mention `sbrassociation.org`; treat that as legacy text, not the preferred link target.

Only first-party material is included:

- SBRA pages on `sbrassociation.com`
- the SBRA-owned **Small Business Resource Show** on its official Libsyn host
- the Apple Podcasts listing linked directly by the SBRA website
- the official Vimeo showcase linked by SBRA's Member Presentations page

The official sitemap is the completeness boundary for articles. The visible News page initially shows only ten items behind a **Show More** control, while the sitemap exposes 37 `/blog/` posts and two legacy-format articles, for **39 articles total**.

## Recommended content grouping

- **Learn — Articles:** all 39 items in the article inventory.
- **Learn — Podcasts:** the show landing page plus all 97 episode links.
- **Learn — Videos:** the Member Presentations landing page and its official Vimeo showcase.
- **Support — Get help:** contact details and SBRA request forms.
- **Support — Membership:** benefits, join/apply, chapters, discounts, insurance, phone-system and account/member resources.
- **Support — Policies:** privacy and website terms.

## Completeness, duplicates and cautions

- **Articles:** the sitemap exposes 39 article pages. The News page displays only a subset until Show More is activated. Two valid articles use legacy root URLs rather than `/blog/`; deduplicate by canonical URL, not path pattern.
- **Podcast:** Apple and the Libsyn RSS feed agree on 97 episodes. There are no duplicate GUIDs, episode URLs or exact titles. The Libsyn homepage shows only recent items, while RSS is the complete archive.
- **Podcast freshness:** the show has no episode after 2021-03-22. The two “Recent Member Podcasts” links on the SBRA homepage are January 2021 episodes and are stale as a recency surface.
- **Source spelling:** preserve source titles unless SBRA approves editorial corrections. The feed contains `Gary Siebert` once and `Elvin Rodirguez` once; other episodes use Gary Seibert.
- **Testimonial mismatch:** the homepage testimonial mentions an episode called “Health Coverage Options for the Self-Employed,” but no exact episode title exists. It likely refers loosely to one of the two Evan Markwood health-insurance episodes. Do not create a 98th episode.
- **Historical content:** most 2020 posts concern COVID-era grants, PPP, unemployment, reopening or public-health information. Keep them visibly labeled **Historical / may be outdated** rather than presenting them as current instructions.
- **Authentication:** several “Members Only” links return an HTTP 200 sign-in shell, so response status alone cannot determine whether content is public.
- **Pricing conflict:** the homepage/application and `/join` page currently show different membership tiers/prices. Link to the official page rather than reproducing prices in the app until SBRA confirms the authoritative schedule.
- **Descriptions:** podcast summaries can contain long biographies and markup. Sanitize them and display a short excerpt while retaining the first-party episode link.
